3. Paste the 5 prompts under the style's heading.

Sample story (resources/style-test/sample_story.txt):
An old father stands at the window of his small apartment, watching the street.
On the table behind him, two cups and a plate of fresh bread wait for his grown son.
Outside, rain runs down the glass and a bus stops at the corner, but nobody steps off.
He sits down slowly in his worn chair and holds his phone, afraid to call.
At last a tall young man hurries along the rainy street, and the father smiles and opens the door.
